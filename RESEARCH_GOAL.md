# LoopQwen versus untouched Qwen Base

The project evaluates one trained looped checkpoint against pinned, untouched Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base at ordinary depth. The current research question is whether it can stay within two percentage points of Base overall and deliver materially better recall, executed tool work and time to a correct answer. The general improvement targets remain +5 and +10 points. Results on the two models quantify the effect of the complete training and architecture recipe. ## What the errors locate

The public five-benchmark panel shows 12 Base misses fixed and 10 Base successes lost. On ARC-Challenge three of the six losses also switched from correct to incorrect under unnormalized likelihood: relative motion, an energy transformation and the number of distinct elements in a formula. The sample supports tests of relation and operation selection on other material. Keep the 250 public items evaluation-only. [Per-item score transitions](docs/measurements/middle-depth/direct/repair-evidence.json)

The full-document fit has now completed 62 short free-generation requests. It scored 4/27 openings at each cap (four chat, zero reasoning and zero tools), versus the update-576 parent's 5/27 at each cap. At 512 it gained one rehearsal message, lost one archive decision and lost one parcel clarification. These results fail the short preservation requirement for retaining the new update. Finish the enrolled books and retain their evidence. The separate 512-token within-checkpoint pass diagnostic tests whether another visit changes the actual decisions or only the cost. [Frozen pass diagnostic](research/diagnostics/middle-depth/short-depth-probe.md) · [Masked short grades](docs/measurements/middle-depth/review/direct-full-fit/grades.json)

The book and tool failures share a specific pattern. The parent retrieved the two ages in both editions of Anne but gave neither requested difference. On the tile invoice the full-document child executed a valid calculation on the wrong operands, then carried the resulting 126 into its next calculation. Sixty of 72 tool training examples already supplied a provider response. For a future training comparison, use fresh source tasks that begin before the first action, vary the requested operation on the same operands, execute the call and verify both the delivered answer and the next turn. Keep ordinary chat and general reasoning in the preservation measurements. The existing bounded learner-history correction also missed.
