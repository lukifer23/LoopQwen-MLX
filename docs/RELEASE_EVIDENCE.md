# Evidence required for a stronger recurrent release

## What would count as a genuinely better loop?

Evaluate one deployable checkpoint making real shared-weight recurrent visits against the pinned ordinary-depth Base. The primary product outcome is the fraction of requests receiving a correct, source-supported final answer that satisfies the user's instructions. An explicitly requested action must execute successfully. Count an unfinished or unsupported answer as a failure. Record correct answers, successful actions, final delivery and actual resource costs separately.

For the next broad confirmation, the proposed quality score gives equal weight to four families: source-grounded reasoning, executable tools, ordinary language and instruction following, and reading original documents with at least 131,072 input tokens. Include both unfamiliar numeric and nonnumeric tools, ordinary questions that need no tools, plausible wrong observations, and novel topics and sources. Within a source, average the registered presentations before averaging sources and families. Freeze sampling, weights, scoring, budget and stopping rule before decoding. The score describes that workload. It cannot establish the absence of regressions on arbitrary prompts.

Use **five percentage points of macro improvement** as the proposed minimum material effect. On the independent confirmation require a paired 95% lower bound above zero on aggregate, a nonnegative point change in each family, and simultaneous one-sided lower bounds above −5 points in each family. Size that confirmation using development-only source variation. An interval that is too wide leaves generalization unresolved. Publish every source-level gain and loss and performance on prior Base successes. The previous experiments keep their original thresholds. These margins are prospective project choices and may be tightened before a new enrollment.

Measure time to a correct final answer, completion by deadline, total wall time, first-output wait, physical prompt throughput, decoding throughput and peak memory. Keep the original five-cost target visible. Counterbalance arm order in confirmation and repeat runs on a fixed machine. Recheck exact input-token identities, unobstructed real tool execution, full attention history and offline model restoration on the candidate actually scored. A speed gain cannot buy quality points. A domain missing from a study stays unmeasured.

The initial development decision is one direct loop/Base comparison. A qualifying result opens an independently trained ordinary-depth twin using the same donor, precision, training sources and optimization budget, a second training seed, and fresh-source confirmation. The twin isolates what the extra trainable computation contributed. Deduplicate passages and group by original source, keep confirmation issuers or documents out of fitting, and keep the final confirmation unopened until the recipe is frozen. The serving path must work with ordinary prompts and real tool outputs without access to evaluation labels.

Applied retrospectively to the *two* domains actually measured in the last conversation study, equally weighted supported delivery was **24.3% loop versus 45.8% Base**, a difference of −21.5 points (95% paired source-bootstrap interval −31.9 to −11.1). This count allows a correct supported financial conclusion regardless of whether the model chose to call the tool. Correctly executed arithmetic is tracked separately. It measures one checkpoint on 48 original sources with one identity-masked project reviewer. ## A deployment claim for a specific workload

A model can earn a narrower recommendation when it completes more of the intended user's jobs, or completes them with materially lower cost at comparable quality. Select independent jobs, an acceptable quality margin, the required advantage and a practical deadline before sampling responses. Count the entire job, including real tool execution and the user's follow-up. Charge failed requests the deadline when comparing time to a correct result. The two-job project pilot registered one additional successful job with none lost, or success on all jobs by both models and 20% less time to correct. A replicated deployment claim requires the paired quality lower bound to exceed −5 percentage points. In the pilot both models finished zero complete jobs.
