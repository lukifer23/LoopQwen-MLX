# Learning an internal pointer transition

Registered at the reviewed Phase 4 revision `c4cb640` before any pointer optimizer updates. At 250K targets the second language-model pass lost two traced answers on seed 109. This study asks whether an explicit state label at each application teaches reusable state advancement.

Use seed 109 and all 3,840 training maps once, eight per update. The first 48 updates alternate depths one and two. The others cycle one to three. Evaluate the first 96 development maps at 24, 120 and 360 updates, then all 384 at 480. Smoke milestones become recovery points on the same trajectories. Hold out 384 additional permutations before fitting. Terminal-only middle and full dense models and a frozen middle probe see the requested hop count. The recurrent input omits it. All arms share the rank-eight block adapter initialization where active and the initial 8,192-weight readout. Average CE within each map, then across maps.

At **each** trained depth require accuracy at least 15 percentage points above the strongest of 1/7, the train-fitted majority and the train-fitted start/depth prior as scored on development. Also require the third readout to beat the first on three-hop terminals. Success triggers a dense multi-state control, a recurrent terminal-only control, forced depths four to six and 80 terminal-only continuation updates. Failure triggers exactly one matched 480-update rank-eight re-entry bridge. Reserve larger budgets until a subsequent review.

For evidence of a reusable mechanism require at least 20% entire three-hop trajectories, at least 70% relabel and order consistency, and at least a 15-point correct donor-transition increase over no patch on same-map and cross-map interventions. Require newly traversed, non-wrapping depths four to six to exceed empirical priors by 15 points. Compare terminal accuracy with the strongest terminal-trained dense model, requiring a three-point mean advantage, before a confirmatory or compute-matched claim. Require retained installation thresholds after annealing before language transfer. Keep the confirmation cohort sealed unless these development tests pass.

Use one Metal worker. Profile three physical policies and logical sizes 4/8/16 at depths 1–3. Pick the fastest safe batch-eight policy by summed median latency across depths with a 384-position cap and measured peak below 6 GiB.
