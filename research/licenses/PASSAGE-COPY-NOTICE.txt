The passage-copy study adapts excerpts, author-provided questions and reference
spans from the original Stanford Question Answering Dataset (SQuAD v1.1) and
Question Answering in Context (QuAC v0.2). SQuAD is by Pranav Rajpurkar,
Jian Zhang, Konstantin Lopyrev and Percy Liang. QuAC is by Eunsol Choi and
colleagues. The passage text is from Wikipedia contributors. Both datasets are
distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
The selected material is redistributed under the same license. Source download
URLs and file digests accompany the frozen manifests. Changes include selecting
one question per passage, stripping QuAC's no-answer sentinel, normalizing the
no-answer response and adding a shared task instruction.
