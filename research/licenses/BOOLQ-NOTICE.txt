The edited questions and labels are from the BoolQ annotations in Gardner et al.,
"Evaluating Models' Local Decision Boundaries via Contrast Sets" (Findings of EMNLP, 2020).
https://github.com/allenai/contrast-sets/tree/e2b87316ba7b30093bc9ffbc340e6550cab79f67/BoolQ
The study selects one opposite-label edit and its original per enrolled paragraph.
Dataset terms belong to their source owners. The experiment code is separately licensed.

The transfer material also includes 512 pairs of e-SNLI training hypotheses
by Camburu et al. with their underlying Stanford NLI captions and judgments
by Bowman et al. SNLI is CC BY-SA 4.0 and the e-SNLI repository is MIT.
The training builder maps entailment and contradiction to a yes/no question.
