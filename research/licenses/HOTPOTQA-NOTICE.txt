The distractor-context HotpotQA questions, answers and supporting facts were
released by Yang et al., HotpotQA, EMNLP 2018. Passage text is from Wikipedia.
The questions and supporting facts are CC BY-SA 4.0. The authors attribute
Wikipedia paragraphs to the pages named in each item's titles. The local
subset preserves all ten passages per enrolled item and adds chat formatting.
