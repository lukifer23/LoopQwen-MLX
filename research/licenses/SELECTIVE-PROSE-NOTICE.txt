Selective raw-language study

Public-domain book text comes from Project Gutenberg editions mirrored by GITenberg.
The training editions are Pride and Prejudice (1342), Moby-Dick (2701), On the
Origin of Species (1228) and Walden (205). The independently held-out editions are
The Call of the Wild (215) and The Voyage of the Beagle (944). The material builder
uses commit-specific files and excludes their Gutenberg headers and footers.
Each source URL and SHA-256 is preserved in the frozen data manifests.

CPython documentation is distributed under the Python Software Foundation
License. A copy accompanies the existing retained technical corpus at
data/v3_retention/PSF-LICENSE. The new material uses other named files from the
pinned CPython revision 4a5632fbf9bf59477c540e3f53fa7cdbeea3e3f5.

The independently held-out science paragraphs are support passages from the
Allen Institute for AI's SciQ test split at revision
2c94ad3e1aafab77146f384e23536f97a4849815. Earlier fitting data also contain
SciQ training support and SQuAD encyclopedia passages as recorded in the
original raw-distillation provenance. Only natural text is used for next-token
prediction. The fitted and held-out token sequences have a 24-token exclusion.
