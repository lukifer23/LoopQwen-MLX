WikiText-2 by Merity, Xiong, Bradbury and Socher uses Wikipedia articles.
The retained 257-token excerpts identify the article title, publisher split
and starting token position. Tokenization uses the pinned Qwen Base tokenizer.

The raw distillation corpus also uses training-set text from SQuAD 1.1
(Rajpurkar et al., CC BY-SA 4.0), SciQ (Welbl, Liu and Gardner, CC BY-NC 3.0)
and the project's attributed CPython and public-domain literary anchors.
The 2B teacher and 0.8B student are official Apache-2.0 Qwen checkpoints.
The SciQ source passages and the derived training set retain the source's
noncommercial terms. No teacher checkpoint or new trained model is pushed.
