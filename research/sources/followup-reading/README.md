# Two consecutive questions from the original QuAC dialogues

The original students asked the second question after receiving the original teacher’s answer to the first. The local source keeps the article title, section heading, background, complete section text, both original questions, all original teacher replies, and every independently annotated text span and offset from the validation set. The two questions of a dialogue always stay together. [QuAC project](https://quac.ai/) · [Choi et al., EMNLP 2018](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1241/)

The source sampler orders dialogue IDs by a fixed hash. It registers 24 distinct development titles and then 128 different training titles. It excludes the titles and passages in the project's earlier passage-copy, document-evidence, conditional reading and natural-context cohorts listed in the sampler. It rejects identical passages and near-duplicates between any two new sources using five-word overlap. It verifies every annotator's original character range, including the published no-answer sentinel. No model generations influence enrollment. Run `.venv/bin/python -m scripts.followup_sources` against the two original v0.2 downloads identified in originals.json.

The original QuAC data are credited to Eunsol Choi and colleagues, and the passages to Wikipedia contributors. This selected material is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. We selected the first two consecutive requests, retained the original span offsets and removed the terminal corpus sentinel from the text displayed to the model.
